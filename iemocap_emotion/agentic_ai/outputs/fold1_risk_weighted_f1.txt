INTERPRETATION
------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Standard MacroF1 treats all classes equally when averaging.
  Risk MacroF1 (mean) still macro-averages but each class's
    per-class F1 is already penalised by its weight in the recall
    denominator — so high-risk classes drag the score down more
    if they have high FN rates.
  Risk MacroF1 (wtd avg) additionally weights the class F1s by
    risk score at the macro-average step, double-penalising
    high-risk classes. Use the mean variant for comparability
    with standard Macro F1 in papers.

  A positive delta in RiskF1 - StdF1 for a class means the model
  happens to have better precision than (weighted) recall for that
  class, so the harmonic-mean numerics shift in its favour under
  the new weighting. A negative delta is the more common case:
  high-risk FNs increase the denominator, pulling recall down.
